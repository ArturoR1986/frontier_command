## Synthesis choice

Considered: large-army RTS with light worker needs; deep individual-room colony simulation with occasional defense; compact civic expedition strategy. Choose the third: one community of up to 16 named people organizes care and work while restoring three distant basin installations. Tactical groups use those same people and provisions. A prepared expedition therefore competes with current construction, food and recovery.

The first implementation's deterministic state, physical inventories, terrain and tests remain useful. Its limited social organization and passive post-establishment goal do not fully satisfy revision 0.2. Keep the tested core, implement the coupled civic/expedition systems, and reassess its art/readability in crowded scenes. This is an evaluation of existing code against evidence, not a claim that the old prototype was the prescribed design.
