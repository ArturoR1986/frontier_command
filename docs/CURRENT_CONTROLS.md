# Current development controls — v0.2

- Click a person, structure or resource to inspect it. Drag empty ground to select a group.
- Right-click to move selected units; movement can withdraw them from combat. Attack-move holds to engage. Mobilize suspends a civilian's ordinary jobs; Return to work releases them.
- Choose a building below the map, then click a clear explored site. **Drag walls** to draw a connected stroke; **drag floors** to fill a rectangle. A stroke is capped at 144 tiles. The preview estimates costs and obvious blocked tiles; the server also checks access and skips invalid sites with a reason. Materials are delivered physically after planning. Leave doorways in walls.
- Lay floors before furniture; beds and work buildings can be placed above existing flooring. Select a plan to cancel it; delivered materials remain recoverable.
- Wheel zooms at the pointer. WASD or arrow keys pan. Shift-drag or middle-drag pans. F or Center colony returns to a usable home view. Whole map shows the full region; click the minimap to reposition. Expand workspace hides the sidebar.
- Escape or right-click during placement cancels the building tool. X stops a selected group. Soil shows fertile ground; Excavate mountain designates an exposed rock face for miners.
- People & work controls priorities. 1 is highest; — disables a role. Research explains missing requirements and offers Assign to study for each eligible civilian. A researcher still needs food, rest and time to walk to the study hall.
- World & expeditions compares actual routes, cargo, fuel and time. Vehicles need fit operators. Local region travel and the regional route network are separate scales.
- The field manual includes your recovery key and experimental offline/conflict rules. Store your key privately. Menus do not pause the shared server.

The small frame/network readout is development instrumentation, not a claim that the game has passed its full load or long-session gates.

Parked vehicles in safe areas release hungry or exhausted crews for food and rest. The people and their skills persist; assign rested operators before departure. AI expeditions reserve sufficient supplies before boarding crews.

A departing party may load its own previously unloaded supplies as well as storage goods. This permits withdrawal before building a depot. Other colonies' loose goods are not available for loading. Stop a local server by entering quit and Enter in its console, or Ctrl+C in an interactive terminal.
