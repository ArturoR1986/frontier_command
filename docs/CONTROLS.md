| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left-click | Inspect person, resource, structure or hostile |
| Left-drag | Select settlers in a box |

## People and work

Names, specialties, traits, health, hunger, rest, morale and work state persist. Inspector priorities cycle 0–4: 1 highest, 4 lowest, 0 disabled. Direct orders take priority until the job ends. Invalid routes or lost targets clear safely.

Resources follow travel → work → carry → storage. Construction follows blueprint → material withdrawal → delivery → foundation/frame/systems → operational structure. The inspector reports deliveries and progress. Carried goods are unavailable for general spending. Interruptions preserve cargo or return it for recovery. Cancelled blueprints leave delivered materials on the ground; dismantled completed structures return half their material costs.

Autonomous mining replenishes roughly 120 alloy and 80 biomass plus outstanding construction needs. Direct gathering can exceed these targets. Farmers maintain a population-scaled food buffer. People eat at depots, sleep near homes and heal during rest; powered homes restore rest faster. Night encourages tired settlers to rest earlier. Low food/rest reduces work movement, and starvation eventually damages health.

## Structures

| Building | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Command Hub | Landing storage, four bunks, 8 power |
| Storage Depot | Shorter physical pickup/drop-off routes |
| Habitat | Four beds and faster powered rest |
| Hydro Farm | Tended crops and food harvests hauled to storage |
| Generator | 16 power; sources serve consumers within 11 tiles |
| Workshop | Tools, defense equipment or medicine research |
| Barracks | Train selected settlers as rangers for 12 alloy each |
| Sentry Turret | Powered ranged defense; rock/wall sight lines matter |
| Sensor Mast | More exploration and longer contact warnings |
| Barrier | Blocks movement and fire; leave a gap for access |
| Commons | Powered shared meals restore social connection; one food per visit |
| Restoration Station | Six local power plus a water, archive or signal benefit; built only at a cleared installation |

Power priority is housing/farms, turrets, sensors, barracks, then workshops. Offline structures display OFF. Supply and demand are inspectable. Layout changes hauling distance, work access, power reach, defense and farming suitability. Fertile soil grows faster; the channel slows travel; worn routes gradually speed it.

## Pressure and recovery

Exposure shows people, wealth, power, territory, industry and military contributions. Automatic raids are prevented before 20 simulated minutes and require Exposure at least 35 afterward. Deliberately activating a guarded installation can start a local fight during that opening. First automatic contact is two scavengers; later groups are bounded by population and exposure. The recovery interval prevents continuous attacks.

Warnings give direction, group size and ETA: 90 seconds normally, 150 with a powered sensor. Rally security, check turret power and protect noncombatants. Scavengers target nearby settlers or the hub; wounded or prolonged contacts retreat. Death drops cargo, defeated contacts leave alloy, workers repair with alloy, and resting survivors heal. Free beds plus 20 food permit replacement settlers, up to 16.

Losing every settler stops simulation and prompts a saved-game load or a new landing. Otherwise the game continues as a sandbox after frontier establishment.

## Community and restoration

Open **Community** to plan while time is paused. Assign field duty to suspend civilian work and hold position; then select the person normally and issue map orders. Release resumes the suspended job when valid. Cargo stays with its owner. Critical hunger/rest automatically releases field duty. Drafting an engineer or grower removes that person's civilian output.

An injury below 50 health leaves a wound: work falls to 60%, movement slows and combat damage falls to 65%. Rest stabilizes wounds at 75 health; appoint caregivers to restore capability. A caregiver carries two food from storage to a wounded civilian. Treatment requires travel and work, leaving another job unstaffed. Two caregivers can treat one another after resting. A lone survivor needs another settler to clear a wound.

Cycle each person's home assignment through available hubs/habitats, four assigned residents each. Home choice affects the journey to rest and gives a morale benefit; destroyed homes lose that benefit. Social connection decays, and people visit a powered Commons below 50 connection. Meals consume one food and restore 40 connection. Low morale reduces work. Treatment and loss leave personal memories visible in Community.

Balanced charter uses normal rates. Industry gives 25% faster work at 60% more fatigue and 40% more hunger. Recovery gives 20% slower work, 30% lower fatigue and a 70% care/rest multiplier. These choices persist and can change at any time.

Use **Basin installations** in Community to jump to expedition planning. **Locate on map** shows a marked site; move settlers there to scout it. The Spillway has no defenders, Survey Archive has two, and Longwatch has three. **Engage defenders** commits to the disclosed fight. Clear the site, then choose **Restore**: workers deliver 40 alloy and 20 biomass before completing the station. Keep civilian workers and routes safe. Each station supplies six local power; Spillway speeds farms 25%, Archive doubles research, and Longwatch adds 60 seconds to contact warnings. Destruction removes that site's ongoing benefit until rebuilt.

Restore all three, maintain a powered Commons, at least four unwounded people and 55% average connection to earn **Independent Basin**. This is an achievement, not a forced ending; settlement development continues. There is no need to wait for a scheduled raid before expeditions.
