FIELD MANUAL

Make the basin a home.

Settlers work autonomously by priority. Give direct orders when it matters. Watch cargo leave a deposit and reach storage before the resource counter rises.

Select / group
Left-click a settler, building or resource. Drag a box for a group. Shift-click adds to selection. The roster also selects people.
Orders
Right-click ground to move, resources to gather, blueprints to assist, damaged structures to repair, or enemies to attack. X stops; Escape cancels placement.
Camera
WASD / arrows pan. Middle-drag or Shift-drag pans. Wheel / + / − zoom. F centers the colony. Click minimap to navigate.
Time
Space pauses. Use 1× / 2× / 4× to accelerate. Menus pause automatically.
Build
Choose a structure below; green tick means valid ground. Leave paths around your buildings. Materials are fetched from depots and delivered before construction begins.
Care
Habitat → Farm → Generator. Food is eaten at storage. Settlers rest near homes; powered habitats restore rest faster. Invite settlers when you have beds.
Power
Consumers must be within 11 tiles of a hub or generator. Homes and farms receive power before defenses and industry.
Frontier
The first 20 minutes are safe. After that, sufficient Exposure attracts scouts. Warnings give direction and time to prepare. Build sensors and turrets, rally Kei, repair afterward.
Progress
Build a Workshop and choose tools, defensive equipment or medicine. Barracks train selected settlers. Open Community to organize field duty, caregivers, homes and the work charter. Scout and restore three marked basin installations for permanent benefits and an independent settlement. Deliberately engaging a site can start combat during the protected opening.
